Lexikon
relacional

Lexikon relacional is an exercise developed by students and faculty in the class “Creative Acting in the Global South and North.” It is a small suite of interactive visualizations for a decolonial glossary of 72 German terms drawn from six academic sources. The project presents this shared vocabulary in several complementary ways: as a deck of term cards organized by letter, as a relational graph linking each concept to its related terms, and as a “constellation” that places the six source texts around the edges while the terms gravitate toward the sources in which they appear — clicking a term reveals its definition and the specific fragments quoted in each text. Together, the pieces turn a static concordance into an explorable map of how these decolonial concepts circulate and connect across the literature, making visible the dialogue between knowledge produced in the Global South and North.
